18 — Findings

English as a Practical Need

  • English is seen as an important and necessary language.
  • Students have different reasons for learning English.
  • English can be useful for:
    • Education
    • Future careers
    • Communication
    • Entertainment
    • Intercultural understanding
  • English is more than just a school subject.
Think about it

What is the most important reason for learning English for you?

19 — Findings

Beyond the Practical View

  • Learning English is not only about practical benefits.
  • English also has:
    • Cultural dimensions
    • Social dimensions
    • Political dimensions
  • Learners should think about the wider role of English.
  • Critical awareness can help learners understand English more deeply.
Think about it

Should English learners think about the cultural and social effects of English? Why?

20 — Implications

Implications of the Study

  • English learners need more awareness of the wider role of English.
  • English should not be viewed only as a tool or commodity.
  • Learners can actively participate in the global use of English.
  • Learners should not only consume English-language cultural products.
  • English can be used to communicate and share learners' own ideas and cultures.

Final Takeaway English: A Practical Need or More?
